Cover Design
Cover shows a group of Malaysian Buddhists paying homage at an ancient stupa located on a cliff at Vrang village in Wakhan Valley of Tajikstan. This site was once a Buddhist complex during 5th-7th centuries AD. Chinese pilgrims Fa Hsien and Xuan Zang were said to have visited Vrang during their travels. Across the valley are the Hindu Kush Mountains of Afghanistan.
