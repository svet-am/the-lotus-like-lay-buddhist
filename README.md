# the-lotus-like-lay-buddhist
Digital Transcription of the book "The Lotus-Like Lay Buddhist" by Bro. Chan Khoon San

# About this repository
This book of dharma was aquired at Buddhist Maha Vihara (https://www.buddhistmahavihara.org/) in Kuala Lumpur, Malaysia.

# Reproduction and distribution
The physical form of this book was printed and distributed free of charge as a gift of dhamma. This digital version is intended as a free gift of dhamma.
